Wireless Headphones

Rs. 2,999

Description

Comfortable over-ear wireless headphones with clear sound, a long-lasting battery, and a built-in microphone for calls. Great for music, study, and travel.

The soft memory-foam ear cushions stay comfortable through long listening sessions, and the adjustable steel-reinforced headband fits most head sizes without pinching.

Bluetooth 5.3 gives a stable connection up to 10 metres, and the headphones pair with two devices at once, so you can switch from your laptop to your phone without reconnecting.

A single charge delivers up to 40 hours of playback. A quick 10-minute charge over USB-C gives about 5 hours, which is handy before a commute.

Active noise reduction softens traffic, fans and background chatter. A transparency mode lets you hear announcements without taking the headphones off.

The built-in dual microphones filter wind and background noise, so your voice stays clear on calls and online classes.

The ear cups fold flat and swivel, and the headphones come with a hard travel case, a USB-C charging cable and a 3.5mm audio cable for wired use.

Backed by a 12-month warranty. If something goes wrong, our support team will repair or replace the unit.

Care tip: wipe the ear cushions with a slightly damp cloth, keep the headphones in the travel case, and avoid leaving them in a hot car.

In the box: headphones, hard travel case, USB-C charging cable, 3.5mm audio cable and a quick-start guide.
